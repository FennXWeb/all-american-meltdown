# Canada, streaming and vehicle effects

Canada begins at world X=1,280,000 cm (north). Checkpoints are every 512 metres along the border, with the first at Y=6,400 cm. The red no-entry line is 250 cm south of the border, behind the human officers and sentinel. Approach an officer and use the normal interact key to talk.

## Entry and customs

- A permanent passport costs exactly **1,000,000 credits**. Buying it again never charges twice.
- Check weapons and ammunition at customs before each visit. Weapon identity, rarity, attachments, finish, chamber/cylinder state and linked magazines are preserved.
- Nearby travelling vehicles' cargo and RV compartments are inspected as well.
- Return to an officer on the American side to collect stored equipment. If storage is full, the remainder stays in customs until there is room; it is never dropped or discarded.
- Passport, clearance and escrow are part of the existing save-slot and checkpoint state.
- Armed vehicle turrets are disabled in Canada.

Unauthorized aircraft receive a radio alert and an on-screen warning, a second warning after ten seconds, and interception after twenty seconds. Returning south cancels interception. Passport holders must clear weapons at customs before flying north.

## Northern region

Follow the northbound highway approximately three kilometres to **Maplehaven**. An officer can mark its position on the route planner. The city has a kilometre-wide street network, clinics, outfitters, cafes and apartment blocks, furnished ground floors, street lighting and roaming residents. Long, curving highways continue through evergreen wilderness.

American enemy tables, roaming bosses and random encounters are excluded from the northern region. Pursuing American enemies are removed if they cross into Canada.

Canadian merchants pay twice an item's base value (four times the ordinary half-price resale rate). Their purchase prices remain higher than resale even with barter perks. Exclusive supplies:

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| Northern Trauma Kit | Restore health and stamina fully |
| Maplehaven Trail Meal | Fill hunger and restore 30 thirst |
| Alpine Recovery Tonic | Fill thirst and stamina; restore 25 health |

## Streaming implementation

`LWStreaming68` moves deterministic road/site planning and terrain sampling onto a bounded worker queue. Workers own their generation settings and caches, and return only value data. Completed neighborhoods are published to gameplay's cache to avoid recomputing the same region on the game thread.

The game thread advances terrain, roads, individual POIs, furnishing batches, surface uploads and population separately. Per-archetype mesh/material bundles load asynchronously before construction, including enemy models. Mesh instances are uploaded in bounded groups. Terrain and procedural surfaces cook collision asynchronously during travel; explicit blocking loads keep collision ready before returning control. Surface uploads track dirty batches. Road markings are clipped to the current chunk before tessellation. A four-second travel prediction loads ahead, and old chunk residents/components retire incrementally.

Interior dressing advances by at most eight placement samples per step with a 1.5 ms soft budget. Towers build one floor at a time while preserving their persistent object IDs. Parked cars and ordinary enemy/civilian populations are created individually, after floor collision is ready. Underground complexes are built behind the loading screen on first entry, rather than during surface travel; their entrances remain visible. Loading a saved underground position reconstructs its interior immediately. Distant Canadian residents suspend navigation until the player approaches. Asset streaming has a separate 2 ms engine budget.

Individual legacy POI construction functions still execute on the game thread. `StreamMaxStep68` and verbose `STREAM68` logs expose their longest indivisible stage; they must be profiled rather than assumed to meet the frame budget.

## Explosions

Vehicle destruction spawns a separate, short-lived effect: turbulent fire billboards, expanding smoke, ballistic sparks, a ground dust ring and a fading flash light. Effects can outlive the source vehicle, are capped at eight simultaneous explosions, and clean themselves up after ten seconds. Loaded wrecks do not replay the blast.
