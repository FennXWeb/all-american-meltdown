## Branch ledger

- **Bellwether:** three fuel allocations; heater repair; optional warning, medical and gate preparation; defend or evacuate; actual Lena health/death; protect or lose intake records; intact, damaged or abandoned aftermath. Preparing the clinic changes the rescue cost and bleeding rate. A living search team must reach the desk to destroy the book.
- **Syracuse:** cooperate over the pump, share its use or use force; return equipment; aid registration with an explicit warning; refuse, falsify, investigate, then retain, recover or destroy dispatch records. The household trail identifies Tomas and his family.
- **Rome:** cargo lien, crew agreement, ordered maintenance, processing belongings, then central/shared/rival dispatch. Retained logistical capacity and shortages feed later operations.
- **Guard:** release Tomas or trace his convoy; obtain restraint and duty-book evidence; private Rook cooperation, public Imani leadership, concealment, or a fragmented hospital guard after Rook dies.
- **Tug Hill:** real severe weather; shelter preparation; observation, warning or ambush combat; supply-backed admission, ration agreement/refuge work, or onward travel; Simon plus family, a recorded account, surrender, or death with an unverified recording.
- **Crossing:** patient station, confidential evidence transfer, and ferry arrangements are alternatives. Each has a separate American-side crisis and a fallback to route selection. Patient/ferry rescue groups physically move to cover. Canadian processing uses the inventory escrow, not a narrative flag pretending the gun disappeared.
- **Toronto:** quiet arrival, workshop/prescription work, tracing and loss, independent investigation, then stay or voluntary return. Mara's return requires earned trust and a defined medical role. Her decision persists.
- **Homecoming / Meridian:** visible Bellwether condition; specific labor identities; publish/private/withhold/sell evidence; invitation, exchange, insider or service-gate entry. The audience offers questions, refusal, a provisional commission or deception. Commission acceptance alone does not execute anyone.
- **Resistance:** coalition responsibilities, divided or faction-controlled reserve keys, prisoner exit, surrender/combat, winter-store preservation/destruction, Keene's fate, physical Carrier defense encounter, human Richardson fight, evacuation and conditional epilogues.
- **Collaboration:** safehouse search, a trusted meeting turned into arrests, individually written former-ally confrontations, permanent execution or explicit defection, Bellwether incorporation, and a blockade against the actual surviving resistance cast. A takeover needs independently secured personnel, reserve control and leverage. Later defection retains deaths, arrests and lost trust.

## Persistence and scene rules

Campaign state is part of the existing RPG save and mission checkpoint payload. It contains scene cursor, decisions, applied rewards, named health/deaths, defeated encounter IDs, journal facts, chapter completion, and ending. No separate save format or runtime JSON loader was introduced.

Dialogue preserves the first-person camera. It gates attacks, holds movement, animates existing faces/gestures, moves speakers to authored marks, and restores input when interrupted. Continue advances a beat; it never selects a response. Pausing retains the cursor. Resuming from far away routes the player back instead of remotely playing the conversation. Missing audio remains subtitled; no gibberish or new paid generation is requested.

## Environment and performance limits

The user approved a limited Rome–Tug Hill–Watertown–Thousand Islands–Adirondack corridor built with existing assets. These are fictional story facilities in compressed regional travel space, not surveyed replicas. Existing Syracuse, Destiny USA, Toronto, landmarks and sandbox systems remain.

Bellwether is a dedicated service-plaza conversion. The market clinic occupies an existing Destiny storefront. Later stops reuse current hospital, warehouse, house, cabin, parking and resort parcels, with story dressing. Toronto processing uses the reserved central green. Meridian's transport hall is an additional industrial set with an armored command platform, office props, isolation control, shutter and escorts.

The production pass adds physical hand contact, treatment/repair routines, restrained seating and case transfers; individually routed evacuation groups; an actual moving ferry with saved journey state; a bespoke Carrier mesh set and authored destruction; animated canal machinery; and dedicated school/residence and Canadian tracing layouts. First-person dialogue still uses the existing subtitle/input system and facial animation. New recordings are not included.

Evacuation completion waits for surviving civilians to reach the receiving point. Deaths and route progress persist; all-casualty cases can advance without resurrecting civilians. Repairs pause while the assisting character approaches and interrupt when the player leaves. Furniture remains after its interaction is completed. Ferry processing uses the existing real inventory escrow and does not replay confiscation or rewards when reconstructing the vessel.
