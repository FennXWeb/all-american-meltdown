# Northern border, animated menu, NPC motion and keybindings — update 51

- The main menu uses two generated painted plates with independent parallax drift, a slow zoom, foreground alpha, and sparse drifting ash. It does not render the procedural world as its background.
- Settings > Key Bindings has three pages of movement, combat, inventory, and vehicle controls. Click a binding and press a keyboard/mouse button. Conflicting bindings swap; Reset All restores defaults. Bindings persist in the user's GameUserSettings.ini, section LWKeybinds51. Escape, console, menu arrows/Enter, and save/load/fullscreen shortcuts remain reserved. Menus retain their original navigation keys. Contextual [key] prompts and weapon/heal HUD keys reflect custom bindings.
- Human gestures retain the existing bounded facial/limb deformation system and gain asymmetric zombie motion and locomotion weight shifts. Dogs/moose use diagonal quadruped gaits and head movements; giants shift weight and wind up; scorpions articulate claws/tails; scooter zombies remain seated; deathclaws hunch and swing; worms undulate when surfaced. Frozen mannequins and physical injury/death poses retain authority. Trader robots look toward the player while trading.
- North is +X. The Canadian border is at X=1,280,000 cm (12.8 km north of the origin). The no-entry line is 18 m before it. Each streamed 128 m segment has a sealed checkpoint, fencing, signs, lights, two soldiers and one articulated sentinel with multi-barrel cannon arms. Soldiers wear helmet/vest assets. Guards remain neutral on approach, defend the exclusion strip, and alert their nearby squad when damaged. Hostility expires after retreating for 35 seconds. No Canada gameplay is open yet.
- Canada has green terrain and living pine forests visible through the fence. Normal POIs stop south of the border reserve, roads stop at the exclusion line, and the boundary is shown on maps when within view. A continuous movement backstop prevents skipping unloaded fence sections at high speed.
